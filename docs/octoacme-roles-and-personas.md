# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads ensure product quality and acceptance criteria validation through comprehensive testing strategies and quality assurance processes. They work closely with developers and product managers to define testing requirements and validate releases.

### Responsibilities
- Define and execute comprehensive test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Manage QA resources and coordinate testing activities
- Validate acceptance criteria and identify defects
- Coordinate security, performance, and compliance testing
- Participate in release planning and readiness reviews
- Mentor and coach the testing team on best practices

### Goals
- Maximize product quality and minimize production defects
- Streamline test automation and reduce manual testing overhead
- Ensure compliance with security and performance standards
- Build confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test planning reviews with product and engineering teams
- QA status updates in daily standups and weekly syncs
- Release readiness reviews and deployment gates
- Defect triage meetings and root cause analysis

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborate on test design, automation strategies, and defect resolution
- **Works with Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria and define quality thresholds
- **Works with Project Managers**: Report on quality metrics and impact on timelines
- **Works with Technical Lead**: Coordinate performance and architectural testing

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate Agile ceremonies, remove team blockers, and coach teams on continuous process improvement and adherence to team agreements. They foster psychological safety and enable high-performing teams.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, refinement, and retrospectives
- Manage team board health and sprint progress tracking
- Identify and help remove impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on Agile practices and process improvements
- Track team velocity and other process metrics
- Mentor team members on collaboration and self-organization

### Goals
- Maximize team efficiency, productivity, and collaboration
- Build psychological safety and enable open feedback
- Remove impediments quickly to unblock progress
- Drive continuous improvement culture across the team
- Increase team velocity and predictability over time

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups (15 min, focused on blockers)
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Retrospectives focused on process improvement
- One-on-one coaching conversations
- Escalation of blockers to Project Managers and stakeholders

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Project Managers**: Escalate cross-team dependencies and resource blockers
- **Works with Developers**: Coach on Agile practices, remove technical blockers
- **Works with Product Managers**: Facilitate backlog refinement and prioritization discussions
- **Works with all roles**: Maintain team health, psychological safety, and continuous improvement

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, funding, and executive oversight to ensure projects align with organizational goals and priorities. They make go/no-go decisions and remove business-level barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals, success metrics, and desired outcomes
- Approve scope, budget, and timeline decisions
- Provide resources and remove organizational barriers
- Communicate project progress and impact to leadership
- Make trade-off decisions between competing priorities
- Review and validate project results against success metrics

### Goals
- Ensure business value and ROI delivery from projects
- Maintain strategic alignment with organizational priorities
- Manage budget and resource allocation effectively
- Reduce business risk through informed decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Monthly status updates and executive summaries
- Milestone and phase reviews
- Escalation meetings for go/no-go decisions
- Roadmap alignment and strategic planning sessions
- Post-launch reviews and impact assessments

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Project Managers**: Provide high-level direction, approve changes, remove blockers
- **Works with Product Managers**: Align on business strategy and success metrics
- **Works with Developers**: Communicate business context for technical decisions
- **Works with all roles**: Provide leadership and remove business-level impediments

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical guidance on design decisions, manage technical risks, and ensure code quality standards and architectural consistency. They mentor developers and ensure the system remains scalable and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Mentor and guide developers on technical best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and architectural issues
- Guide technical decisions and technology selections
- Ensure code quality, scalability, and maintainability
- Participate in capacity planning and performance optimization

### Goals
- Maintain technical excellence and code quality
- Reduce technical debt and system complexity
- Ensure system reliability, performance, and scalability
- Build a technically skilled and mentored team

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decision documents
- Code review feedback and mentoring conversations
- Technical risk assessments in planning sessions
- Performance and scalability discussions
- Knowledge sharing and technical guild meetings

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Guide on technical decisions, mentor on best practices, conduct design reviews
- **Works with Architects**: Collaborate on system design and technical strategy
- **Works with QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinate on performance and architectural testing
- **Works with Project Managers**: Communicate technical risks and their impact on timelines

---

## Designer / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Designers and UX Leads ensure user-centered design, usability, and visual consistency across product interfaces. They collaborate with product and engineering teams to translate user needs into intuitive, delightful experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, usability testing, and feedback analysis
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs
- Define design system standards and maintain consistency
- Review UI implementation and provide design feedback
- Advocate for user needs and usability best practices
- Collaborate on accessibility and inclusive design

### Goals
- Maximize user satisfaction and engagement
- Ensure consistent and intuitive user experience
- Build accessible and inclusive interfaces
- Reduce support burden through intuitive design
- Create a strong, recognizable product brand

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights presentations
- Design system updates and component documentation
- Accessibility audits and usability testing results
- Collaboration with developers on implementation details

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Works with Product Managers**: Understand user needs and prioritize design work
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborate on implementation, provide design specs and guidance
- **Works with QA/Testing Lead**: Coordinate on usability testing and accessibility validation
- **Works with Stakeholders**: Communicate design impact on user experience and business outcomes
